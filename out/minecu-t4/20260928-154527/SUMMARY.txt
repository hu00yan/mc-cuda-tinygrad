READING THIS OUTPUT
  * The three selftest_* entries gate everything. If any says FAIL, the
    numbers below it are not trustworthy and the harness is the suspect.
  * Each entry also gates on a DDA step count. An entry that reports a
    throughput but a mean of ~1.000 steps/pixel is NOT a voxel-render
    measurement: the render exited on its first voxel test every pixel.
    bench.cu refuses to report timings for such a config unless
    --measure-degenerate is passed, which is used exactly once here, for the
    entry that exists to document the pre-fix baseline.
  * env-steps/s is batch / median_us. A whole-step number, not a per-kernel one.
  * The measured memory floors in each log are the denominators. %-of-floor
    figures only mean something against a floor measured on the same card.
  * The author's 49.6M is an RTX 3090 claim. This is a T4. They are not
    comparable numbers and must not be differenced directly.

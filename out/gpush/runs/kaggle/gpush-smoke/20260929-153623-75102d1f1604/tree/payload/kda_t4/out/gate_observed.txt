5. one real forward, then the config autotune actually picked
============================================================================
  calling fla.ops.kda.chunk_kda  B=1 T=128 H=2 HV=2 K=128 V=128 dtype=torch.bfloat16  grad off
  inputs: the STUDY's own generator (kda/kda_vs_fla.py:296 `rnd`), with
  q,k L2-normalised. Raw Gaussians make the delta-rule matrix
  (I - beta k k^T) have eigenvalues near 1 - K*beta and the state
  diverges; that is a property of the generator, not of fla.
  dtype: torch.bfloat16 is the only option. The FlashKDA verifier requires bf16
  (flash_kda.py:64-88) and the study's call shape is bf16
  (kda/baseline_pin.py:392). fp16 would be a different payload type
  with a different numerics story, not a free substitute.
  returned o: shape=(1, 128, 2, 128) dtype=torch.bfloat16 finite=True  absmax=6.1523e-02

  Autotuner.cache has 1 entries after ONE call:
    key=(2, 2, 128, 128, 64, False, 'torch.bfloat16', 'torch.bfloat16', 'torch.bfloat16', 'torch.bfloat16', 'torch.float32', 'torch.bfloat16')
      -> BV=32 num_warps=4 num_stages=1  (from a space of 4)

  the config space was the SHORTENED one, and autotune picked out of
  exactly that short list. There was no refusal anywhere in this run.

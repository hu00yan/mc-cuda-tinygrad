WHAT TO READ
  * A framework emits a graph of kernels; the 4.55 M baseline is a
    whole-step number over 7 launches. Dividing one by the other is a
    category error. The defensible comparison is render against render at
    the same batch, and the batch table above is the same-card, same-run
    version of that.
  * Every timing is printed next to the DDA step count of its own run.
    A throughput number without the workload behind it is not a
    measurement; see minecu_ref/buried_agent_fix.md for what that costs.
  * No number here is transcribed from any notes file. Every figure is
    produced by an instrument in this notebook on this card.
